## Overview
Cyberborn is an open-world 3D cyberpunk survival game developed in Unreal Engine 5.7. Utilizing the AAA assets from the Cyberpunk MEGApack Environment Bundle, the game drops players into a vast, rain-slicked neon metropolis where they must gather energy cores, craft shelter/defenses, and survive against rogue synthetics patrolling the streets.

## Key Features
* **Survival & Crafting Mechanics**: Implemented survival attributes (hunger, thirst, shield energy, and stamina) paired with an intuitive modular crafting and grid-based base-building system.
* **Environment Bundle Integration**: Integrated the Cyberpunk MEGApack Environment assets using customized PBR master materials, volumetric rain/fog, and interactive neon signs to establish a rich, rain-soaked sci-fi atmosphere.
* **Optimization for Open World**: Achieved playable framerates by leveraging Unreal Engine 5.7's **Nanite** virtualized geometry and **Lumen** global illumination, along with aggressive Hierarchical Level of Detail (HLOD) and custom draw distance tuning.
* **AI Rogue Synthetics**: Programmed combat-ready AI enemies using Behavior Trees and Blackboard states, allowing them to patrol, investigate disturbances, search for players, and coordinate attacks.
