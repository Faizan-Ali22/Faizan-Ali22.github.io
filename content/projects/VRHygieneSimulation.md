## Overview
Developed an immersive VR simulation focusing on environmental cleanliness standards and hygiene workflows using **Unity XR** and the **Oculus SDK**. The experience trains users through realistic contamination detection, procedural sterilization steps, and hygiene protocols in a fully interactive 3D virtual environment.

## Key Features
* **Interactive Hygiene Workflows**: Implemented step-by-step procedural training loops covering surface sanitization, PPE donning/doffing sequences, and bio-waste disposal adhering to international cleanliness standards.
* **Physics & Direct Hand/Controller Interactions**: Leveraged Unity XR Interaction Toolkit and Oculus SDK to program realistic 6-DoF grabbing, spray nozzle mechanics, and surface-wiping interactions.
* **3D Environmental Assets**: Modeled and textured interactive props, sanitization equipment, and clinical stations using **Blender** and **Adobe Photoshop**, keeping polycounts optimized for standalone VR headsets.
* **Spatial Feedback & UI**: Integrated spatialized 3D audio, haptic controller pulses upon successful disinfection, and non-intrusive diegetic world-space UI guides.
* **Standalone VR Optimization**: Achieved locked framerates on Meta Quest hardware through static batching, low-draw-call material atlasing, and lightweight baked lighting.
